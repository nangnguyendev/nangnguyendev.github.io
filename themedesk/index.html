Theme Desk

Themes, wallpapers, and widgets for your Mac desktop.

Add a widget

  1. Control-click your desktop and choose Edit Widgets.
  2. Search for Theme Desk and add a widget.
  3. To give one widget its own look, Control-click it and choose Edit.

Restore a purchase

Open Theme Desk, choose Get Theme Desk Pro from the app menu, then click Restore Purchases.
