Theme Desk Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Theme Desk ("the app") personalizes your Mac desktop with themes, wallpapers, and widgets. We don't collect your personal data, and the app has no accounts, ads, or analytics.

Information stored on your Mac

Your chosen looks, custom widget text, countdown events, weather city, and widget photo are stored only on your Mac. They are never sent to us.

Weather

If you add a Weather widget and choose a city, the app looks up that city using Apple Maps and requests its forecast from Apple WeatherKit. Those requests go to Apple, not to us. The app doesn't ask for your current location.

Calendar

If you connect your calendar for the Up Next widget, the app reads upcoming events on your Mac to show them in the widget. Calendar data stays on your Mac and is never sent to us. Declining keeps the rest of the app working, and you can change this in System Settings.

Photos and folders

The app only reads an image or changes the icon of a folder that you choose yourself. Chosen images are resized and stored on your Mac for the Photo widget.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We don't receive your payment information. The app checks your purchase status with Apple's StoreKit.

Children

The app is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
