What you can do

  • Care schedules: for 260+ houseplants, herbs and vegetables, adjusted for light, pot, soil and season.
  • Reminders and widgets: a daily to-do list, notifications and Home Screen widgets.
  • Plant ID and light meter: identify a plant and measure a spot's light with your camera, on-device.
  • Plant doctor: describe a problem and get a treatment plan from Apple's on-device model.
  • Weather alerts: storm, frost and heat warnings for outdoor plants.
  • Journal: photos and notes to track growth.

Free for up to 5 plants and 3 plant IDs a day, with ads. Greenkeep Pro adds unlimited plants, unlimited plant ID and the plant doctor, and removes all ads.

Common questions

Where is my data?
On your iPhone, in the app's private storage. There's no account and no Greenkeep server, and the developer can't see your garden.

Do ads or analytics see my plants?
No. Ads come from Google AdMob and usage analytics from Firebase. Neither receives your plants, photos, notes, doctor chats or location. Turn analytics off at Settings › Privacy › Share usage data.

How do I change ad or tracking choices?
Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices in Greenkeep (where the law requires it), and iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

How do I remove ads?
Subscribe to Greenkeep Pro in Settings. Already subscribed on another iPhone? Tap Restore purchases.

Reminders don't arrive.
Check iPhone Settings › Notifications › Greenkeep is on, then open Greenkeep once so it can reschedule.

How do I cancel Pro?
iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Greenkeep, at least 24 hours before the period ends.
