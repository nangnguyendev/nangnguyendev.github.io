Greenkeep Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026 (updated)

Summary

Greenkeep is a plant care app. Your plants, photos, notes, care history and plant doctor chats stay on your iPhone. There's no account and no Greenkeep server, and the developer can't see your garden. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob and its partner ad networks, and the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which features are used. Neither ever receives your plants, photos, notes, chats or precise location. Greenkeep Pro removes all ads.

What stays on your iPhone

Your plants, spots, schedules, care history, journal photos and videos, notes, plant doctor chats and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone, and shared with the Greenkeep widgets on the same device. The app doesn't upload them anywhere. Your iPhone's own device backup may include app data, under your Apple Account settings.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to take plant photos, identify a plant and measure light with the light meter. Plant identification runs on your iPhone. Light readings are processed on your iPhone and never saved. Photos you choose from your library are copied into the app's private storage and never sent.

Location and weather

Optional. If you allow location, Greenkeep requests local weather from Apple Weather (WeatherKit) to adjust watering for the season and warn you about storms, frost and heat. Apple handles that request under its privacy policy. Your location is not sent to ads or analytics by Greenkeep.

Plant doctor

The plant doctor uses Apple's on-device Foundation Model. Your questions, photos and the plant's details are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to the developer, to ads or to analytics.

Advertising

The free version of Greenkeep shows ads from Google AdMob, which may fill them through partner networks (AppLovin, Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, InMobi and Moloco). Ads appear as a card in the Today, Garden, plant library and symptom lists, a banner on the plant library and plant pages, occasionally a full-screen ad at a natural break (for example after adding a plant, finishing the day's tasks or closing a plant identification; spaced out and capped per day) and occasionally when you open or return to the app. You can also choose to watch a video ad for one more free plant slot, more plant identifications today or a day of the plant doctor. Ads never appear during setup, purchases, the camera or a plant doctor chat.

To show and measure ads, Google and these networks may collect a device identifier, approximate location from your IP address and how you interact with ads. Nothing you enter about your plants is passed to any ad SDK. Where the law requires it, including US states with privacy laws, Greenkeep asks for your consent first, and Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices lets you change it. Greenkeep asks your permission before ads may track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, ads aren't personalized. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Usage analytics

Greenkeep uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count which screens and features are used (for example, that a plant was added, a task completed or the plant doctor asked a question), how often the app is opened, purchases and ad impressions and ad revenue. Events come with a random app instance ID, not your name or Apple Account.

Analytics never includes your plant names, nicknames, photos, notes, doctor chats, spots or location. Ad personalization signals from analytics are turned off. You can stop analytics anytime at Settings › Privacy › Share usage data.

Purchases

Greenkeep Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the developer never sees your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your iPhone.

Notifications

Care reminders are scheduled on your iPhone as local notifications. No push server is used.

Your choices

  • Delete your data in Settings › Your data, or delete the app.
  • Stop analytics with Settings › Privacy › Share usage data.
  • Change ad consent with Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it) and tracking in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Remove all ads with Greenkeep Pro.
  • Google's analytics and ad data is handled under Google's policies; the developer has no copy of your garden to delete.

Children

Greenkeep isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
