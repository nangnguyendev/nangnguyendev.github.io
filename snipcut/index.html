Common questions

Are my videos uploaded?
No. Editing, subtitles and every smart tool run on your iPhone. Videos, photos, music, recordings and text are never uploaded.

What does Snipcut Pro include?
All templates and no ads, as a one-time purchase through your Apple Account. Every editing tool is free and exports have no watermark.

I bought Pro on another device.
Open Settings › Snipcut Pro and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I change ad or tracking choices?
Settings › Privacy › Ad Privacy Choices in Snipcut, and iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Turn off usage data and crash reports in Settings › Privacy › Share Usage Data.
