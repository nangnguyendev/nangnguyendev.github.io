Snipcut Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Summary

Snipcut edits your videos, photos and collages on your iPhone, and your media is never uploaded. There's no account. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Snipcut uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which features are used. Neither ever receives your videos, photos, music, recordings, text or project names.

Your media and projects

Videos, photos and music you choose, voice recordings and drafts stay in Snipcut's storage on your device. They're never sent to the developer. Deleting a draft or the app removes them. Exports are saved to your photo library or shared only when you choose to.

Permissions

  • Photos: to open the items you pick and save your exports.
  • Microphone: to record voiceovers when you tap Record.
  • Speech Recognition: to turn speech into captions and find filler words.
  • Music Library: to add songs you choose.
  • Notifications: to tell you when an export finishes.

Each is asked for only when you use that feature, and the rest of the app keeps working if you say no.

Apple services

Smart tools use Apple frameworks that run on your iPhone (Vision, Core Image, Speech, Translation, speech synthesis). Speech is recognized only on your device; languages your iPhone can't recognize offline aren't offered. Translation may download language files from Apple. Auto Zoom finds where faces are in a clip on your iPhone and keeps only the zoom keyframes it creates; it doesn't identify anyone and stores no face data.

Advertising

Ads never appear while you edit, export, record or buy. You may see a banner on Home, Templates and the Guide, one ad card in your Drafts, a full-screen ad after an export or save or when you go back to Home from an editor (spaced out and capped per hour), and occasionally one when you return to the app after a while. You can also choose to watch an ad to use a Pro template once. They come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect a device identifier, approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it, Snipcut asks for your consent first, and Settings › Privacy › Ad Privacy Choices lets you change it. Snipcut asks your permission before ads may track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, ads aren't personalized. Snipcut Pro turns ads off. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Analytics

Snipcut uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count which features are used (for example, that a video was exported at 1080p), how often the app is opened, ad impressions and purchases. It never includes your media, text, project names or anything you type, and it isn't used to track you across other apps unless you allowed tracking. If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report with the device model, iOS version and the name of the open screen, never your media or text. You can turn both off in Settings › Privacy › Share Usage Data.

Purchases

Snipcut Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the developer never sees your payment details or Apple Account. Snipcut asks the App Store, on your iPhone, whether Pro is unlocked and keeps that answer on the device. If you share usage data, the purchase (product, price and currency) is counted in analytics.

Feedback

Send Feedback prepares a message that you choose where to send. Only what you share is sent, and it's used only to answer you and improve the app.

Children

Snipcut isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on the App Store page, with a new effective date.
