GoodCart Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 (updated for age range and ad reports)

Summary

GoodCart is a grocery barcode scanner. Your health profile, family, allergies, avoid list, scan history and lists stay on your iPhone. There's no account and no GoodCart server, and the developer can't see what you eat. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count which features are used. Neither ever receives your profile, family, allergies, diets, avoid list, the products you scan or your questions to the assistant. GoodCart Plus removes all ads.

What stays on your iPhone

Your goals, diets, allergies and avoid list, your family members and their profiles, scan history, favorites, shopping list, weekly goal, reminder settings and assistant conversations are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone, and shared with the GoodCart widgets on the same device. The app doesn't upload them anywhere. Your iPhone's own device backup may include app data, under your Apple Account settings.

Product lookups

To look up a product, GoodCart sends the barcode you scan or the text you search to Open Food Facts, a non-profit open food database. These requests don't include your profile or any identifier from GoodCart. Open Food Facts handles them under its privacy policy.

Camera, location and the assistant

  • Camera: reads barcodes and Nutrition Facts labels. Images are processed on your iPhone and are never uploaded or saved.
  • Location: optional, used only while you look at nearby grocery stores, through Apple Maps. GoodCart doesn't store it or send it to ads or analytics.
  • Assistant: runs on your iPhone with Apple's on-device Foundation Model. Your questions are not sent to the developer, to ads or to analytics.

Advertising

The free version of GoodCart shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on product pages, a card on the Today screen, occasionally a full-screen ad after you close a scan result, and occasionally when you open or return to the app (spaced out and capped per day). You can also choose to watch a video ad to see every swap for one product. Ads never appear during onboarding, in the camera, during a shopping run, on the purchase screen or in the assistant.

To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect a device identifier, approximate location from your IP address and how you interact with ads. Nothing about your health, family or the products you scan is passed to any ad SDK. GoodCart does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). Where the law requires it, including US states with privacy laws, GoodCart asks for your consent first, and Profile › Privacy › Ad privacy choices lets you change it, including opting out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Age range

Where state law requires age assurance (for example Texas, Utah and Louisiana), GoodCart asks iOS for your age range through Apple's Declared Age Range feature. GoodCart only receives a range such as "18 or older", never your birthday, and keeps it on your iPhone. It is used to turn off analytics and show only child-safe ads for users under 13, and to limit ads to non-personalized, teen-appropriate ones for users under 18 or anyone who chooses not to share.

Usage analytics

GoodCart uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the app is used: for example that a scan was completed and from where (camera, typed or label), that a swap was opened, an item added to the list, a reminder opened, the paywall shown, a purchase or renewal, and ad impressions and ad revenue. Events come with a random app instance ID, not your name or Apple Account.

Analytics never includes your profile, family, allergies, diets, avoid list, product names, barcodes or assistant questions. Ad personalization signals from analytics are turned off. You can stop analytics anytime at Profile › Privacy › Share usage data.

Purchases

GoodCart Plus is sold through the App Store as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes the payment; the developer never sees your payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple on your iPhone. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Notifications

Streak reminders, the Sunday recap, shopping reminders, daily food questions and feature tips are created on your iPhone as local notifications. Each kind can be turned off in Profile › Reminders. When you open a notification, analytics records only which kind it was, never its content.

Health information

GoodCart gives general nutrition information and is not medical advice. The health details you enter (allergies, diets, goals, avoid list and family profiles) are consumer health data under laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act. GoodCart keeps them only on your iPhone and never collects, shares or sells them.

Your choices

  • Delete your data by deleting the app. Remove single scans by swiping them in Lists › History.
  • Stop analytics with Profile › Privacy › Share usage data.
  • Change ad consent or opt out of sale/sharing with Profile › Privacy › Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it).
  • Report an inappropriate ad with Profile › Privacy › Report an ad.
  • Remove all ads with GoodCart Plus.
  • Google's analytics and ad data is handled under Google's policies; the developer has no copy of your health data to delete.

Children

GoodCart isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from them.
