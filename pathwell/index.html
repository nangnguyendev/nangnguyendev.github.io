Pathwell Health

A private daily health log for iPhone. Medicines, food, water, weight, habits and how you feel, in one place. No account. Your logs stay on your iPhone.

Email support Privacy Policy

What you can do

  • Medicines: a dose calendar, reminders, injection sites, dose changes and refills, with an illustrated level chart for GLP-1 medicines.
  • Food: log meals by search, voice, barcode, label or photo. Protein, fiber and calorie goals, recipes and a 7-day meal plan.
  • Water, weight and activity: one-tap logging, trends and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.
  • How you feel: side effects, habits and daily challenges, with patterns from your own logs.
  • Pulse: an estimate from your iPhone camera and fingertip.
  • Your doctor: a PDF report to bring to your next visit.
  • Apple Health: optional two-way sync.

Every feature is free. Pathwell shows ads, which never use your health logs.

Common questions

Where is my data?
On your iPhone, in the app's private storage. There's no account and no Pathwell server, and the developer can't see your logs. Export an encrypted backup from Profile to keep a copy.

Do ads or analytics see my health data?
No. Ads come from Google AdMob and usage analytics from Firebase. Neither receives your doses, medicines, weight, meals, symptoms or anything else you log. Turn analytics off at Profile › Share usage data.

How do I change ad or tracking choices?
Profile › Your data › Ad privacy choices in Pathwell (where the law requires it), and iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Reminders don't arrive.
Check iPhone Settings › Notifications › Pathwell is on, then turn the reminder on again in Profile › Reminders.

How do I move to a new iPhone?
Create an encrypted backup in Profile, save it to Files, and restore it on the new iPhone with the same password.

Is Pathwell a medical device?
No. Pathwell is a personal log. It doesn't diagnose or treat any condition and doesn't suggest doses. Pulse readings are estimates. Check with your doctor before making medical decisions. In an emergency, call 911.
