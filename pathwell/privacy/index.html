Pathwell Health Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Summary

Pathwell is a private daily health log. Your medicines, doses, symptoms, weight, water, meals, habits, pulse estimates and readings stay on your iPhone. There's no account and no Pathwell server, and the developer can't see your logs. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and it uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which screens and features are used. Neither ever receives anything you log.

What stays on your iPhone

Your prescription, doses, side effect scores, weight, water, meals, habits, pulse estimates, device readings and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone. The app doesn't store them in iCloud. Your iPhone's own device backup may include app data, under your Apple Account settings.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used only while you measure your pulse, scan food or take a meal photo. The microphone is used only while you log a meal by voice. Video and audio are processed on your iPhone and never saved or sent. A photo you choose is read on your iPhone and not stored.

Food lookup (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a barcode you haven't scanned before, Pathwell sends only that barcode number to Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org), a non-profit open food database. When you log a meal by voice or photo, it sends only the food names it recognised (for example "greek yogurt") to search.openfoodfacts.org. Your recording and photo are never sent. Package and label photos load from images.openfoodfacts.org. Open Food Facts receives the request like any website visit, including your IP address, under its own privacy policy. Turn this off at Profile › Look up foods online.

Apple Health

Optional. If you connect it, Pathwell saves your weight, water, meal nutrition and device readings, and reads your weight, water, device readings, steps and heart rate. Apple Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sent to analytics, and never shared.

Advertising

Pathwell shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on Recipes and Meal plan, one ad card on Today, occasionally a full-screen ad after you save a meal or activity (spaced out and capped per day), and occasionally one when you open or return to the app. Ads never appear while you log a dose, measure your pulse, set up the app or while the app is locked, and never right after you open a dose reminder.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect a device identifier, approximate location from your IP address and how you interact with ads. Ads are never targeted using your health information, and nothing you log is passed to the ad SDK. Where the law requires it, including US states with privacy laws, Pathwell asks for your consent first, and Profile › Your data › Ad privacy choices lets you change it. Pathwell asks your permission before ads may track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, ads aren't personalized. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Usage analytics

Pathwell uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count which screens and features are used (for example, that a meal was logged by barcode or that the medicine calendar was opened), how often the app is opened, and ad impressions and ad revenue. Events come with a random app instance ID, not your name or Apple Account.

Analytics never includes your doses, medicine names, weight, meals, foods, symptoms, readings, pulse, notes or any other value you log. Ad personalization signals from analytics are turned off. You can stop it anytime at Profile › Share usage data.

Files you create

Encrypted backups and PDF reports go only where you choose to save or share them. A PDF report is not encrypted.

App lock

You can lock the app with Face ID or your passcode. iOS handles this, and the developer never sees your face data or passcode.

Purchases

Pathwell has no in-app purchases. Every feature is free.

Your choices

  • Delete everything on your iPhone with Profile › Erase all data, or delete the app.
  • Stop analytics with Profile › Share usage data.
  • Change ad consent with Profile › Your data › Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it) and tracking in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Google's analytics and ad data is handled under Google's policies; the developer has no copy of your logs to delete.

Consumer health data (Washington, Nevada and other states)

Pathwell does not collect, share or sell consumer health data. Your health logs stay on your iPhone. The usage and ad data described above doesn't include health information.

Children

Pathwell isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes
