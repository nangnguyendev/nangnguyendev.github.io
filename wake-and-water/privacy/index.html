Wake & Water Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Wake & Water ("the app") is an alarm clock and water tracker for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information stored on your device

Your alarms, wake-up history, water log, custom drinks and settings are stored only on your iPhone. The Home Screen widget reads a summary of today's water from a shared container on the same iPhone. We do not run servers for the app and we do not receive this information. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in the app:

  • Saved to Health: the water and caffeine you log, so they appear in the Health app.
  • Read from Health: your sleep, only to show how long you slept. The app does not read any other health data.

Health data stays on your iPhone and in Apple Health. It is never sent to us, never used for advertising or analytics, and never shared with third parties. You can change access anytime in the Health app › Sharing › Apps › Wake & Water. Data the app saved to Health stays in Health if you delete the app; you can remove it in the Health app.

Permissions

  • Alarms (AlarmKit): lets the app schedule alarms that ring while your iPhone is locked, silenced or in a Focus.
  • Notifications: water reminders and goal updates, scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Camera: used only during the Barcode Scan mission to read the code you chose, and during the Drink Water mission to check that you're holding a cup. Images are analyzed on device by Apple's Vision framework and are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Motion & Fitness: used only during the Shake and Steps missions to count shakes and steps. This data is not stored or shared.

Every permission is optional. If you decline one, the related feature is unavailable and the rest of the app keeps working.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, the advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used to personalize ads. You can change this anytime in your iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. In both cases you can review your choice anytime in the app under Settings › Privacy › Privacy Choices.

If you use "Report an ad", the app opens an email to us with the reason you chose, the ad's type, placement and time, any note you add, and the app version. Nothing is sent unless you send that email.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app performs: app opens, purchases and subscription status, and ad impressions and revenue. It does not include your alarms, water log or any Health data. Analytics are turned off for users under 13.

Age range

Where Apple's Declared Age Range feature applies to you (for example in places whose laws require age checks), the app asks iOS for your age range. We receive only a range (under 13, 13–15, 16–17 or 18+), never your birthdate, and it stays on your iPhone. If you're under 18, ads are limited to content suitable for teens and are not personalized, and the app doesn't ask for tracking permission. If you're under 13, ads are child-directed and analytics are turned off.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment information. The app checks your purchase status with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone.
  • Turn off Health access, notifications, camera or motion in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Change ad choices in the app under Settings › Privacy › Privacy Choices.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's data practices change, and update the effective date above.
